What ships with the app
-----------------------

is output of Kokoro-82M v1.0 (hexgrad, Apache-2.0); attribution is recorded in
every manifest.json ("licenses") and in Kokoro-82M-Apache-2.0.txt. No model file,
voice file or tool binary is distributed.
